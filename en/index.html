Q&A
What is this weird site (and why did I even stumble here)
This is the OFFICIAL website of the VIDEOBOOK/indie animation project "Different Light: Ultimate Diodes and Their Life Cycle". One big big deep story about diodes, solo developed by Ukrainian artist and animator Surzik25.
Who is Surzik25
It's me, the developer of this site. Hi :D
Why haven't I heard of this author before
Because he (she) didn't make herself known. I've been developing this project in absolute obscurity for six years, not letting anyone peek over my shoulder. But now.. I think I'm finally ready to share it with everyone who has an open heart.
Why does this site even exist
For reading! All pages that have been officially released are published here (except for very new ones, because they haven't been released yet). Click the "read" button next to any of the blocks and let's go!
WHY IS IT LAGGING SO MUCH
We don't have the opportunity to get our own server yet, so you can sometimes be scared by various unpredictable lags and bugs (especially on mobile devices xD). If you suddenly have panels running or an unpleasant blinking cursor, take it bravely. The chaos ends with a simple page refresh.

at least, it should.
Is this HANDMADE?
Of course! Different Light is a videobook developed WITHOUT any participation of generative AI. Everything is pure and natural, dear sir. AI still can't draw diodes, let alone animate them. The REAL artist remains the winner :D
A videobook? Are you serious?
Yeah. It couldn't be more serious ;3 It's like a comic book from the future or a weird visual novel, but not interactive.
Where did this come from
from an other-dimensional hole I'll answer like an adult: it was (and is) a daily, multi-year journey of work, nerves, pain, doubts, adrenaline, ups and downs, but at the same time, crazy love. While I was sitting in the shadows, I had plenty of time to work everything out..not gonna lie :3
Where did you get so much time
It was my voluntary choice. After graduation, my heart screamed a big "NO" to higher education, and the outbreak of war in our country was my last sign. I stayed home and tried with all my might to escape from reality, fulfilling my old dream - to become a good animator. And I escaped so far that I came here.
Do you work anywhere else?
No. Different Light is my full-time job. At the time of writing this, I receive practically no income from it, but it is difficult to overestimate the joy and meaning it has given me.
No way. You are a faker.
No, I'm not. What's the point of pretending to be someone I'm not? Especially with the risk of being exposed? If you want proof - join Discord, you won't regret it. It's fun there :)
Who are Diodes
Here they are :D The creatures you see on the covers. They might seem a little strange to you, but..you know what? We humans would seem strange to them too if they saw us. They're from another planet, that's all.
What is this book about
(no spoilers) These are the diaries of two teenage diodes: Klocha (Line 3) and Per-pere (Line 4). They live on opposite ends of the half-formed Diode Planet near the universal Abyss, each telling stories about their daily lives with their aunts, animals, mechanisms, side thoughts, and strange stories that haunt them.
This sounds like a story for children. Nothing interesting.
Hmmm, really? My dear skeptical adult friend, this is just the beginning. I can't wait to get going and do Lines 1 and 2 - that's where the heat starts >;D
When will you do Lines 1 and 2
Spoiler: not soon. I'm currently busy developing those same Lines 3 and 4, but storms are heading there too. So stay tuned :3
Where are the new pages?
Honestly, I'm not very efficient at updating the site one page at a time, so I need to do a whole chapter (or at least a half of it) first. The new pages will definitely come as soon as I get this pile of work done, but it will take a while :D
Is there a way to read the new pages?
Yes! I've been dreaming of creating a Patreon for a long time, where I can give super early access to new pages and other exclusive materials (currently Line 3 Chapter 3 and Line 4 Chapter 4). If it's not difficult for you and brings you positivity, please join immediately! I'll be happier than you even think!

It's not the same as paying big studios or corporations, where five dollars doesn't even count as a contribution - it's a little different. I mean, completely different.
